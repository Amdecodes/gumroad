# ArifDrop Strategic & Architectural Decisions

**Project**: ArifDrop (Gumroad fork for Ethiopia)  
**Date**: October 2026  
**Auditor**: Antigravity Assistant  
**Repository Baseline**: `antiwork/gumroad` (`cc4d616c7`)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

ArifDrop adapts Gumroad’s proven creator e-commerce platform specifically for the Ethiopian digital economy.

Our guiding engineering philosophy is **Preserve Existing Functionality by Default**: Gumroad represents over a decade of production hardening, edge-case handling, and battle-tested domain modeling. We do not re-architect or delete core capabilities unless there is a concrete, irreconcilable barrier to Ethiopian operation (e.g. non-functional payment rails, foreign tax APIs, or unconvertible currency constraints).

---

## 2. Core Strategic Decisions Summary

| Domain                       | Strategic Decision    | High-Level Rationale                                                                                                            |
| :--------------------------- | :-------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Product Types**            | **Broad / All**       | Support all native product types (digital, physical, courses, memberships, bundles, commissions, newsletters, podcasts, calls). |
| **Marketplace**              | **Discovery Enabled** | Retain `/discover` marketplace for organic search, categories, and staff picks to bootstrap local creator discovery.            |
| **Payments**                 | **Chapa Integration** | Replace US-centric card checkout with Chapa (supporting Telebirr, CBE Birr, Awash, local cards).                                |
| **Currency**                 | **ETB Primary**       | Promote Ethiopian Birr (ETB / Br / ብር) to primary catalog pricing and balance holding currency.                                 |
| **Subscriptions**            | **Launch Supported**  | Support subscriptions at launch, adapting recurring billing to **manual monthly renewal**.                                      |
| **Marketing Suite**          | **Full Retention**    | Retain broadcast emails, drip workflows, abandoned cart recovery, and audience segmentation.                                    |
| **Storefront Customization** | **Full Page Builder** | Retain customizable seller profiles, rich text blocks, and custom CSS/HTML capabilities.                                        |
| **Payouts**                  | **Manual Initially**  | Payouts to Ethiopian banks and Telebirr will be disbursed via administrative batch export / manual transfers initially.         |
