## 5. Resolved Core Terminology Decisions

The following terminology decisions have been confirmed and applied as a single global replacement across `app/javascript/locales/am/*.ts`, `config/locales/am.yml`, and `GLOSSARY.md`:

### 1. "Creator" vs. "Seller": Standardized on ሻጭ (Seller / Merchant)

- **Decision**: Standardized on **ሻጭ** (plural: **ሻጮች**, possessive: **የሻጭ**, definite: **ሻጩ**).
- **Rationale**: Removes inappropriate theological connotations of **ፈጣሪ** (traditionally reserved for God / the Divine Creator in everyday Amharic).
- **Applied**: Global replacement completed across checkout notices, creator ratings (`የሻጭ ደረጃ`), admin tabs (`ሻጮች`), library filter (`ሻጭ`), content access notes, and creator profiles (`ከፍተኛ ሻጭ`).

### 2. "Tip" vs. "Discount": Standardized on ጉርሻ vs ቅናሽ

- **Decision**: **ጉርሻ** is reserved strictly for optional buyer tips/gratuity, and **ቅናሽ** / **የቅናሽ ኮድ** strictly for discounts and promo codes.
- **Applied**: Verified distinct across checkout, storefront, and discover filters.

### 3. "Payout": Standardized on የሻጭ ክፍያ (Seller Payout)

- **Decision**: Standardized on **የሻጭ ክፍያ** (singular) / **የሻጭ ክፍያዎች** (plural).
- **Rationale**: Replaces ambiguous general terms **ክፍያዎች** ("payments") and **የገንዘብ ክፍያ** ("money payment") to clearly distinguish seller disbursement transfers from buyer incoming payments.
- **Applied**: Global replacement completed in `admin.ts`, `am.yml`, and documentation.

### 4. Brand Names Script (ቻፓ vs Chapa)

- **Status**: Payment provider names like Chapa and Telebirr remain consistent with brand guidelines, keeping English script in decline errors ("በ Chapa ይክፈሉ").

### 5. Seller-Facing Scope (Dispute Strings)

- **Note**: The dispute evidence strings (`am/disputes.ts` and `config/locales/am.yml:267-277`) are seller-facing, not buyer-facing. They are retained safely for Phase 4 dashboard work.
